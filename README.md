## What is Fathom?

Cross-border business fails in predictable ways. A founder brings the wrong gift. A buyer says "no" too directly. A CEO skips the small talk. The problem isn't ignorance — it's that **nobody tells you what matters until you've already made the mistake.**

Fathom is an AI tool that generates a complete cultural playbook from your scenario. Type *"I'm meeting a Japanese department head tomorrow"* and in seconds you get: opening greetings in Japanese, what to wear, what never to say, a meeting agenda, risk warnings, and a dialogue simulator to practice before you go.

Not generic tips. Scene-specific guidance generated by AI, grounded in 594 verified cultural rules.

---

## AI Pipeline

Fathom runs an AI analysis pipeline every time you submit a scenario:

| Stage | What Happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **Country Detection** | AI identifies the target culture from your scenario description |
| **Cultural Anchoring** | Pulls structured data: Hofstede scores, GLOBE practices, Trompenaars dimensions, 594 verified rules |
| **Scene Analysis** | DeepSeek v4 Flash generates a scene-specific playbook: briefing, taboos, dos, strategy, risks, deliverable |
| **Web Enrichment** | Tavily API searches in real-time for industry context and sanctions compliance |
| **Dialogue Generation** | AI creates bilingual practice dialogues with cultural scoring |
| **Progressive Rendering** | Content streams to the browser as it's generated — no blank screen |

Built with parallel AI calls and structured fallback. If one response is incomplete, another fills the gap.

## What Makes It Different

### 🎯 Scene-Specific, Not Generic
Every culture has 50 ways to say "no." But the way a Japanese department head says it over green tea is different from how a Brazilian buyer says it over coffee. Fathom's AI analysis is anchored to your actual scenario.

### 🗣️ Practice Before You Go
The AI-powered simulation engine scores your dialogue on cultural fit, communication effectiveness, face preservation, and language quality — then gives you a better version.

### 🌍 Structured Database, Not Hallucination
594 verifiable cultural rules. Hofstede, GLOBE, Trompenaars data. The AI augments this foundation; it doesn't generate from thin air.

### 📊 Compare Any Two Cultures
Select two countries → AI-powered clash detection across 12 dimensions → specific behavioral recommendations.
